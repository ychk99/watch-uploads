Privacy policy

Watch uploads. Last updated: 21 November 2026.

Who runs this app

Watch uploads is a personal app, run by its owner for their own watch and their own Google account.

Who uses it

Only the owner signs in to the app. It has no other users.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives Google's permission to see, edit, create and delete files in the owner's Google Drive. It uses that permission only to add new recordings from the owner's watch to the owner's own Drive folders, and to read the file lists of those folders to avoid duplicates and to check that each recording arrived complete.

What it stores

The Google sign-in is kept only on the owner's watch. Recordings go only to the owner's own Google Drive. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

What it does not do

The app never deletes, moves or changes files in Google Drive. It does not collect personal information about anyone. It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all uploads.

Changes to this policy are posted on this page.
